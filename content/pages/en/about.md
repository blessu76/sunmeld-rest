Sunmeld (subtitle: Space Merge Duel) is a merge duel where you play against an AI opponent. It is made by **ipe()** (ipe Studio) and is **in development** for iOS and Android. There is no store page or release date yet.

## How to play

- You and the AI take turns dropping space stones into one shared tub.
- When two stones of the same stage touch, they merge into the next stage, from dust up to a sun.
- The player who causes a merge scores it.
- If the tub overflows, the player who placed the last stone loses.

## Opponents

You always play against an AI opponent. Each opponent has its own habits, so the same tub plays out differently each time.

## Price and ads

Sunmeld is planned as free to try, with a single purchase that unlocks the rest. There are no ads.
