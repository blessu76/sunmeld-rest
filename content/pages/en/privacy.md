**In short:** the Sunmeld app collects no personal information. This website collects basic visit statistics through Google Analytics, remembers your language in your own browser, and asks for no name, email or account. Neither shows ads, and we do not sell data.

Effective date: {{EFFECTIVE}} · Operator: {{OPERATOR}}

## 1. Information we collect

Sunmeld ("the App") is a mobile game operated by an individual developer, {{OPERATOR}}. The App has no accounts or sign-in, and it does not collect or send to any server any personal information such as your name, email, contact details, location or device identifiers. Your game progress and settings are stored only on your device and are deleted when you delete the App.

The App contains no advertising, analytics or tracking SDKs. The website is different: it collects basic visit statistics through Google Analytics 4, as described in section 2.

## 2. The website

The sunmeld.ipe.rest website ("the Site") has no accounts or sign-in, shows no advertising, and does not ask for your name, email or contact details.

### Visit statistics (Google Analytics 4)

We use Google Analytics 4 to see which pages are read. It records page views, the page you came from, your browser and device type, an approximate location (country or city) and clicks on links that leave our site (the link address only). It sets cookies in your browser (such as `_ga`, which can last up to two years) to tell visits apart. We do not ask for your name or email in analytics, and we cannot identify you from it. Google processes this data on our behalf, which can include servers outside Korea.

### Stored in your browser

When you pick a language on the home page, we may save that choice in your browser's local storage (under the name `sm-lang`). It stays on your device and is never sent to us. You can clear it in your browser settings. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, so ordinary web request information (such as your IP address) may be sent to Google.

### Your choices

- Block or delete cookies in your browser settings, or use Google's [Analytics opt-out add-on](https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout). The site works the same without them.
- Clear site data to remove your saved language.

### Email you send us

If you write to [{{EMAIL}}](mailto:{{EMAIL}}), we receive your email address and whatever you include. We use it only to reply and to handle your request, and we keep it as long as needed for that. You can ask us to delete it at any time.

### Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any host, it receives technical request data such as your IP address; GitHub's own privacy statement applies to that.

## 3. Third parties

Google (analytics and fonts) and GitHub (hosting) process data for the website as described above. We do not sell your information, and we do not share it with anyone else except where the law requires. The App is distributed through the Apple App Store and Google Play; information related to downloads is handled by each store under its own privacy policy.

## 4. Questions and your rights

To request access, correction, deletion or suspension of processing of your personal information, or to ask any question, write to [{{EMAIL}}](mailto:{{EMAIL}}). We reply within 10 days. The App holds no personal information, and website analytics data is not tied to your name or email, so we usually cannot find an individual in it; email correspondence we can locate and delete.

## 5. Children

The App is not directed to children under 14 (or the minimum age in your region), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The App collects none, and the website asks for none.
