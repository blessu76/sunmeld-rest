**In short:** the Sunmeld app and this website do not collect your personal information.

## 1. Information we collect: none

Sunmeld ("the App") is a mobile game operated by an individual developer, {{OPERATOR}}. The App has no accounts or sign-in, and it does not collect or send to any server any personal information such as your name, email, contact details, location or device identifiers. Your game progress and settings are stored only on your device and are deleted when you delete the App.

The App contains no advertising, analytics or tracking SDKs.

## 2. The website

The sunmeld.ipe.rest website uses no cookies and includes no analytics or advertising tags. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, so ordinary web request information (such as your IP address) may be sent to Google. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, and GitHub's server-log policies apply.

## 3. Third parties

Because we collect no personal information, we have nothing to share with or sell to third parties. The App is distributed through the Apple App Store and Google Play; information related to downloads is handled by each store under its own privacy policy.
