Sunmeld is still in development for iOS and Android. This note is about a setting that existed but could not do anything.

## The switch was there, the sound was not

The settings screen had a sound switch, and it saved your choice correctly. The game checked it every time a stone landed or merged. But the part that should have played the sound was an empty placeholder. We had left it that way on purpose, because the sound files and their license were not decided yet. The switch itself was never broken. What was missing was the sound.

## Making sounds ourselves

Instead of waiting for a license, we made the sounds ourselves. There are sixteen short effects for things like landing, merging, chains and the overflow warning. They are generated by a small script that uses no random numbers, so running it twice gives the same files. Together they are about 220 KB.

## Quiet when your phone is quiet

The game is set up to respect the silent switch on iPhone, and not to stop music from another app that is already playing. On Android it plays as a game sound and does not take audio focus. If a sound fails to play, the round goes on as normal.

## What we have not heard yet

The sounds are wired up and the app builds with them. We have not yet listened to them on a real iPhone, in a real room, at a normal volume. The tone and loudness may need another pass. When that happens, a sound file can be swapped for one with the same name.

## FAQ

### Why was there no sound before?

The sound output was a silent placeholder. The sound files and the audio part of the app were added only recently.

### Does the game respect the silent switch on iPhone?

That is how it is set up. We have not yet checked it on a real device, so we will say more once we have.

### Can I turn the sound off?

Yes. The sound switch in settings controls the effects. When it is off, the game plays nothing.
