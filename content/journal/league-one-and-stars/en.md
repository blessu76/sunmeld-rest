League 1 is now in the Sunmeld test build. Sunmeld is still in development for iOS and Android.

## A league of opponents

In League 1 you play against AI opponents that each have their own habits. You take them on one at a time, and your results are saved.

## Results scored by stars

Each result in a league is scored by stars. The rule for how many you earn is clear and shown in the game, so you always know what a better result would have taken. A win earns a star, and a wider win with a calm tub earns more.

## Why stars

A win or a loss tells you little about how a game went. Stars give you something to improve on after a win, and a reason to come back to an opponent you have already beaten.
