Sunmeld is still in development for iOS and Android. One merge brings you level with the AI. If both scores stay there through the remaining drops, the standard duel rules give the win to the side that just caught up.

## The last points earned

On a tie, the game checks who last earned points. The AI might drop the final stone, but if that drop produces no points for either side, the last scorer stays the same. If a later merge puts either side in front, the higher score decides the match.

## When neither side scores

If a duel ends on points and neither side has scored, it’s a draw. A duel that ends with an overflow or when a Sun forms follows the rule for that ending.

## FAQ

### Who wins a duel that ends on equal scores?

Under the standard duel rules, the side that last earned points wins.

### Does dropping the final stone win a tie?

A drop that earns no points leaves the last scorer as they were. That last scorer wins the tie.

### What if neither side earned any points?

If the duel ends on points, it’s a draw.
