The new screens in Sunmeld are available in 12 languages. Here is how that was done and what is still open. The game is still in development for iOS and Android.

## Choosing a language

The game follows your phone's language list and uses the first language it supports. If none match, it falls back to English. You can also pick a language yourself in settings.

## Translating

The translations were made with an AI tool and rewritten to fit the tone of the game, which is short, light and respectful, instead of being translated word for word. The name Sunmeld stays the same in every language.

## Right to left

Arabic is one of the 12 languages, so the screens also have to work right to left. We check every language on small screens and with larger text so that long phrases still fit.

## What is not done

A native speaker has not reviewed these translations yet. Wording may change, and we would like native speakers to look at the languages with different writing systems before any release. There is no release date yet.
