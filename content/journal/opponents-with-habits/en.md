A duel is only fun if the other side feels like someone. This week's note is about the AI opponents in Sunmeld, which is still in development for iOS and Android.

## Not one AI, but several

Instead of a single opponent that plays the same way every time, we are building a group of AI opponents that each have their own habits. Some like to stack stones tightly and wait for a big merge. Some drop stones fast and keep the tub busy. Some play it safe near the rim, and some take risks.

## Why habits matter

Because the tub is shared, every stone you place changes your opponent's next move, and the other way around. When opponents have different habits, you cannot rely on one trick. You read how this opponent plays, then decide whether to merge now or to leave the tub in a bad spot for them.

## What we are testing

We are playing many rounds against each opponent to check that they feel different but fair, and that a patient player can always find a way to win. Overflow still decides a round: the player who placed the last stone loses.

## What is next

The opponents will keep being tuned as the game takes shape. There is no release date yet. When there is news, it will show up here.

## FAQ

### Who do I play against in Sunmeld?

An AI opponent. There is a group of them, and each has its own habits.

### How do the opponents differ?

Some stack tightly and wait for a big merge, some drop fast, some play it safe near the rim, and some take risks.

### What decides a round?

Overflow. The player who placed the last stone loses.
