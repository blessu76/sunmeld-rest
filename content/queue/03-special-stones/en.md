Sunmeld now has three special stones: wind, frost and hole. The game is still in development for iOS and Android.

## Why add them

A shared tub can get stuck. When it is crowded and every stone is risky, a special stone gives the player a different kind of move. They are part of the later league rules, and each one comes with an explanation in the game.

## The three

Wind pushes stones in the tub, which can open space or change which stones touch. Frost is a stone that works differently from a normal one; the game explains how. Hole clears a stone and eases a crowded tub.

## Fair for both sides

The AI opponents can use special stones too, under the same rules. We have been running many simulated games to check that no special stone decides a round by itself. Those simulations use AI players and are not the same as real people playing, so we are waiting for tester feedback.

## What is next

We will keep watching how special stones feel in real games. There is no release date yet. See the [release notes](/releases/) for test build changes.
