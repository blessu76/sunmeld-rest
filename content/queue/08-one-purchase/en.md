Sunmeld is free to try, has no ads, and has one purchase. This note explains what that purchase is. The game is still in development for iOS and Android.

## What it unlocks

League 1 is free to play. League 2 can be unlocked with a one-time purchase. You pay once and it stays unlocked, with no subscription.

## Restore

The purchase screen has a restore button. If you set up a new phone or reinstall the game, restoring brings League 2 back without paying again.

## What it does not do

Stone shapes and bucket patterns are not part of the purchase. They unlock as you play and nothing about them is sold.

## Testing it

In the test build, testers try the purchase flow with sandbox accounts only, so no real payment is made. We show the price the store gives us and do not set one ourselves. There is no release date yet. See the [release notes](/releases/) for test build changes.
