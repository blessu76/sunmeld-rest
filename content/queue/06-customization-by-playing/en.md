The Sunmeld test build lets you change how the game looks. Sunmeld is still in development for iOS and Android.

## Stones and buckets

You can choose from a few stone shapes and a few bucket patterns. They change the look only. Rules, scoring and physics stay the same.

## Unlocked as you progress

Each choice unlocks as you progress, through stars, leagues and daily challenges. A locked choice shows what it asks for and how far along you are.

## Nothing is sold

Customization is not a purchase. There is one in-app purchase in the game, and customization is not part of it. We would rather give players something to earn than something to buy.

## Not only by color

Choices differ in shape and pattern, not only in color, so they are easy to tell apart.

## What is next

We are still looking for the right pace of unlocking, and we will wait for tester feedback. There is no release date yet. See the [release notes](/releases/) for test build changes.
