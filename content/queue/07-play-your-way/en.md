Some changes are not about rules. This note covers three that make Sunmeld easier to pick up. The game is still in development for iOS and Android.

## Pick up where you left off

A saved game can be resumed right where you left off, so a phone call or a lock screen does not cost you a round.

## Accessibility

The game has screen reader labels and supports larger text. We check that screens still fit at small sizes and with larger text.

## 12 languages

The new screens are available in 12 languages. We wrote the translations ourselves and have not had every language reviewed by a native speaker, so wording may still change.

## What is next

We will keep going through screens for fit and clarity. There is no release date yet. See the [release notes](/releases/) for test build changes.
