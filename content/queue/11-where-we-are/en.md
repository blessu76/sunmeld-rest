This is a short look at Sunmeld as it is today. The game is still in development for iOS and Android, and there is no release date.

## What you can play in the test build

You drop space stones into one shared tub, take turns with an AI opponent and merge stones of the same stage from dust up to a sun. League 1 has AI opponents with their own habits, and results are scored in stars. A short tutorial walks you through the first game, and a saved game can be resumed.

## Around the game

There is a daily challenge, one new game each day that is the same for everyone. You can unlock stone shapes and bucket patterns by playing. Screens are available in 12 languages, with screen reader labels and larger text support.

## Comeback devices

The first move alternates between games and a finale zone adds a last push near the end, so a game stays open to a comeback.

## What is still open

League 2 can be unlocked with one purchase, and that flow is still being tested with sandbox accounts. The translations are waiting for native speaker review. We keep checking screens for fit and clarity. Free to try, one purchase, no ads: that part stays as it is.
