As the jar fills up, it can get harder to see whose stone is whose or which stones can merge. In the settings of the Sunmeld test build, you can adjust outlines and turn on stage numbers to read both details right on the stones. The game is still in development for iOS and Android.

## Read ownership by the ring

Set the outline to “Rival dashed” and rival stones get a dashed ring. Your stones keep a solid ring. In a duel, that gives you a way to tell the sides apart by shape when the colors are hard to distinguish.

## Find matching stages by number

Turn on “Stage numbers” and a small number appears near the bottom of each stone in the jar. Look for matching numbers and see where those stones might touch.

## FAQ

### Which stones get dashed outlines?

With “Rival dashed” selected, the rival’s stones in the jar get dashed rings. Your stones have solid rings.

### Is the number the stone’s point value?

It’s the stone’s stage. Stones with matching numbers are at the same stage.

### Do stage numbers appear in solo stacking?

Yes. When the setting is on, the stones in the jar display their stage numbers in solo stacking too.
