Move a stone from side to side in Sunmeld and a dashed circle moves with it inside the jar. It gives you a place to look before you let go.

## When a stone is in the way

With a clear path, the circle reaches the floor. When another stone is in the way, the circle stops at the spot where the falling stone would first touch it. If you’re aiming between two stones, watch which side the circle catches on.

## After the first touch

The guide uses the size of the stone you’re holding and the current positions of the stones in the jar. Once it touches, the stone can roll sideways or merge with one of the same stage. Try nudging your aim to see where the circle slips from the top of a pile into a gap beside it.

## FAQ

### What does the dashed circle show?

It estimates where the stone would first touch the floor or another stone at your current aim.

### Does the size of the stone change the guide?

Yes. A large stone can touch the sides of a gap that a smaller stone would fit through.

### Will the stone stay inside the circle after the drop?

It can roll or merge after the first contact.
